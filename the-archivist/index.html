About

The Archivist is a practice-based research project comprising and surrounding the development of performance firmware for the Noise Engineering Versio Eurorack platform, with documentation, audio examples and a film collaboration. It proposes a model of sampling as memory, in which captured sound is organised into retention strata and degraded through historically grounded emulations of antiquated tape machines and media and digital samplers.
